# Steel Framing Construction Project - Procore Implementation

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This case study demonstrates how Procore construction management platform can streamline 
a steel framing project in Brisbane, Australia. The implementation focuses on reducing 
project delays, controlling costs, and improving team communication.

---

## 2. PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Project Name:** Brisbane Commercial Steel Frame (Example)  
**Location:** Brisbane, QLD, Australia  
**Project Type:** Commercial Building - Steel Framing  
**Duration:** 6 months  
**Team Size:** 15-20 people (engineers, foremen, tradies, supervisors)

### Current Challenges (Pre-Procore)
- ❌ Scattered communication (email, phone, WhatsApp)
- ❌ Manual budget tracking (Excel spreadsheets)
- ❌ RFI delays (paper-based or unclear email chains)
- ❌ Schedule overruns (no real-time tracking)
- ❌ Document management chaos (multiple versions)
- ❌ Safety concerns (documentation not centralized)

---

## 3. PROCORE SOLUTION

### 3.1 Project Setup & Planning

**Objective:** Establish centralized project structure

**Procore Implementation:**
1. Create Company Profile + Project
2. Add team members (Superintendent, Foreman, Field Staff)
3. Set project phases:
   - Phase 1: Mobilization
   - Phase 2: Steel Erection
   - Phase 3: Connections & QA
   - Phase 4: Inspection & Closeout

**Benefits:**
- Single source of truth
- Role-based access control
- Clear accountability

---

### 3.2 Budget Management

**Initial Budget:** AUD $500,000 (steel framing portion)

**Procore Features:**
- Cost codes for materials (steel, bolts, paint, etc)
- Labor tracking by task/phase
- Real-time budget vs actual comparison
- Cost forecasting to identify overruns early
